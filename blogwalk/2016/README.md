# 2016 — the census

90 posts, 2016-01-11 to 2016-12-23. The largest of the twelve years and 12% of the whole
corpus, within eight posts of 2015 and 2024 combined. Kubernetes 1.1 through 1.5 — four
releases inside twelve months. **Most of this year's nouns still exist and mean something
different**, which is the third staleness case and the reason 2016 was censused after the two
ends:
Deployments arrive, PetSet is introduced *and* renamed to StatefulSet nine months later, RBAC
goes alpha, CRI is proposed, and Minikube, Helm and kubeadm all launch — see
[the method](../README.md).

**Yield: 15 `walk`**, at the ceiling of the [8–15 band](../README.md#yield) and inside it.
34 `read`, 3 `dated`, 38 `skip`.

The ceiling was reached but did not bind. Seventeen posts had a case for an exercise; both that
lost it lost on a rule rather than on space. *High performance network policies* is hands-on,
measurable, and its numbers would move — and it is a vendor post, which the
[genre rule](../README.md#four-genres-with-a-rule-because-they-are-most-of-the-corpus) keeps out
of the exercise set on principle. *Tail Kubernetes with Stern* is a clean, small diff that lost
to fourteen larger ones. Both are `read`, with the reason written into the row.

The three `dated` rows are all storage, all in a seven-week stretch, and each fails on a
different kind of unobtainable. *A Thousand Instances of Cassandra* is scale the post itself
calls its largest ever. *Stateful Applications Using Kubernetes and Datera* needs a storage
appliance — and FlexVolume, the mechanism it uses, is still compiled into the kubelet at the
pin, which makes it the cleanest illustration in the corpus of `dated` being about the hardware
and not the API. *Create a Couchbase cluster* is the softest of the three: one node would fit
under the ceiling and a cluster would not.

The 38 rejects are seven weekly meeting notes, four SIG introductions, three release-series
index posts, two monthly news digests, two conference announcements, a two-part build log for
one CI job, a survey, a birthday post, a partner-program announcement, a Dashboard
release-notes post, a hosted-agent tutorial, and thirteen vendor or ecosystem posts that record
no decision. That is 42% of the year — below 2015's 48% and more than twice 2024's 19% — and
the growth in absolute terms is almost entirely vendor volume rather than noise: 19 posts carry
an `ecosystem` tag against 4 in 2015.

`etcd` is the only tag with no rows. 2016 predates the etcd2-to-etcd3 migration the blog would
later cover, and the storage layer is not discussed this year.
